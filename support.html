Questions and answers

Something missing? Write to info@tactus.works. Replies usually come within a few days.

What is in the On this day deck?
Every photo and video taken on today's date in an earlier year. The deck is built fresh each day. On a day with nothing from past years, the day still counts for your streak.
Does swiping left delete straight away?
No. Swiping only sorts. At the end of the deck you see everything marked for deletion, can rescue single items, and delete the rest in one step. iOS asks you to confirm once. Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
Why does the app want full access to my photos?
Because it has to list, show and delete items across the library. With limited access, Back Then only sees the photos you picked. You can change this at any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos.
Does it work with iCloud Photos?
Yes. Items that are not on the phone are downloaded when their card comes up, so a deck may take a moment on a slow connection. Deleting removes the item from iCloud as well, after the 30 days in Recently Deleted.
What do the Tools decks do?
Recent shows today and yesterday. Screenshots shows your newest 200 screenshots. Biggest first lists your largest videos, so a few swipes free the most space.
Can I change the reminder time?
Not yet. The reminder comes at 20:00 and only on days when the deck is still open. Turn it off in Settings, Notifications, Back Then.
What does the heart do?
It marks the item as a favourite in your Photos library, the same flag you see in the Photos app.
What gets shared when I tap Share?
The original photo or video and a short line of text, in the language of your iPhone. You choose the app in the share sheet; Back Then sends nothing itself.
How do I reset the counters?
Delete and reinstall the app. The freed-space counter, streak and ritual days live only on your phone.
