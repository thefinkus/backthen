Back Then
A daily photo tidy-up for iPhone

One deck a day. Keep or delete. Done in a minute.

Back Then shows you the photos you took on this day in earlier years, one card at a time. Swipe right to keep, left to delete. Nothing is removed until you have seen the review, and deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

  • On this day: the same date, every past year. Finish the deck and your streak grows.
  • Tools: recent shots, screenshots, and the biggest videos first.
  • On your phone only: no account, no upload, no tracking, no ads.

Free on the App Store (link follows once the app is live).
