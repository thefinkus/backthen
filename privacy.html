Privacy Policy

Your photos stay on your phone.

Effective 28 October 2026

Back Then runs entirely on your iPhone. It does not collect, store or send any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. The app makes no network requests.

Photo library

Back Then uses Apple's Photos framework to read your library on the device: to find the photos and videos for each deck, to show them, and to carry out the changes you ask for. Those changes are limited to three things:

  • Deleting the items you marked, only after the review and only after iOS has asked you to confirm. Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album, where iOS keeps them for 30 days.
  • Favouriting: tapping the heart sets or clears the favourite flag of that item in your library.
  • Sharing: tapping Share hands the original file and a short line of text to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is between you and the app you choose; Back Then itself sends nothing anywhere.

The app asks for full access to your photo library because it has to list, show and delete items. With limited access it can only work on the photos you selected.

Data kept on the device

Back Then remembers a few numbers in its own preferences on your iPhone: how much space you have freed, how many items you have deleted, which days you finished the daily deck, and whether sound is on for videos. These values never leave the device and are removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Back Then schedules a local reminder every evening with the size of the day's deck. Local notifications are created and delivered on the device only.

Children

Back Then does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.
